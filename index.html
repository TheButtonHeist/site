More than buttons and labels.

Accessibility exposes controls, values, custom actions, additional content, and navigation through rotors.

Button Heist makes these capabilities available to software, including parts of the interface that screen-based tools cannot reach.

One interface, three uses.

For accessibility users

Button Heist exposes, exercises, and audits the accessibility interface. Developers can find gaps in what people can reach, understand, and operate.

For agents

Button Heist shows what the app exposes and which actions are available. Agents can act on that information and inspect the result.

For automation

A heist describes a workflow and its expected results. Automation can repeat that heist through the same runtime.

Accessibility is a power feature.

A complete accessibility interface gives people more ways to use your app. Button Heist brings that same interface to agents and automation.
